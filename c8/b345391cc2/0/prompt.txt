The share retrieve endpoint is live on :9100 (backend commit 2e6f5688c), and I've checked it independently.

`GET /api/v1/facility/{facility_id}/abdm/shares/{share_id}/` returns a bare object identical to a list row (`id, token_number, counter_code, abha_number, abha_address, profile, status, patient|null, created_date, expires_at`). It finds the share whatever its day, not just today.

Error cases, in the order the backend checks them:
- ABDM disabled: 503 `abdm_disabled`.
- Unknown facility: 404 `object_not_found`.
- No queue permission: 403 `{"detail"}`. This is returned for every share id, whether it exists or not.
- Another facility's share, an unknown id, or a malformed id: 404 `profile_share_not_found`, loc `["profile_share"]`.
