The success path only invalidates `["serviceRequest", facilityId, serviceRequestId]` when observations were part of the batch. But the `update-report` sub-request always runs and writes `conclusion` / report metadata, and `ServiceRequestShow` derives `diagnosticReports` from `request.diagnostic_reports` on that same `serviceRequest`
 query. So saving a conclusion-only edit (no observation values) leaves 
the parent's report list stale — the review card below still shows the 
old conclusion until something else refetches.

Suggest moving the `serviceRequest` invalidation out of the `if` and keeping only the toast inside it.
