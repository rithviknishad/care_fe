**Convention** — this deletes the badge pill from *every* `Callout`
 in the app to serve two new icon call sites. The white rounded-full 
container with the per-variant border was the component's visual 
identity; now `badge` renders bare, so the three pre-existing usages (`CreateScheduleTemplateSheet`, `EditScheduleTemplateSheet` ×2) had to be converted to icons in this same PR just to still look intentional.

Widening `badge` to `ReactNode`
 is the right call and costs nothing. Removing the wrapper is a 
shared-component change made to avoid styling a caller. If an icon badge
 shouldn't get the pill, that's a variant of the badge slot — not a 
deletion of it. E.g. keep the wrapper and let callers opt out, or render
 the pill only for string badges (`typeof props.badge === "string"`).

As it stands this is a design change to a `src/CAREUI`
 primitive smuggled into a supply-delivery bugfix — worth splitting out 
or getting explicit design sign-off, since nothing outside this diff 
asked for it.
